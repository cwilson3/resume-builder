## About Bilbo

**Retired adventurer, occasional burglar, and very dependable host**

Respectable hobbit of Bag End who, much to the neighbors' surprise, once left without a pocket handkerchief and came back with a dragon's treasure, an elven sword, and a great many stories. Known in the Shire for punctual meals and tidy accounts, and beyond it for slipping past goblins, spiders, wood-elves, and one very large dragon. Happiest by the fire with a pipe and a map, but will answer the door to a wizard.

## Talents

- **Burglary:** silent entry, lock assessment, barrel logistics, key acquisition, invisibility (situational)
- **Riddling:** fish, time, eggs, darkness, and the contents of one's own pocket
- **Field Kit:** Sting (elven short sword), mithril corslet, maps with moon-letters, a good walking stick
- **Hospitality:** second breakfast, elevenses, seed-cake, raspberry jam and apple tart, washing up without breaking plates
- **Diplomacy:** hostage exchanges, treasure-share disputes, polite conversation with dragons
- **Travel:** Misty Mountains, Mirkwood, Long Lake, Lonely Mountain, and the road back again
